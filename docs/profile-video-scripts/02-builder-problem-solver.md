That process has taught me something important: it's easy to talk about what technology could do. The real challenge is making it work reliably, safely, and usefully.

Today, I lead QuantumShield Labs, where we're focused on artificial intelligence, automation, security, and what I call governed intelligence.

The idea is straightforward.

As AI agents become capable of performing real work, we need to give them appropriate authority without losing accountability or human control.

Too much access creates unnecessary risk. Too little access makes the technology ineffective.

We're working on finding that balance through practical engineering, testing, clear permissions, and human oversight.
